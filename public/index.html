Where to find it
  1. Open Canvas Calendar.

About Modules and teacher announcements

A Calendar Feed cannot read private Modules or course announcements. Reading those without a PAT requires Canvas OAuth or an institution-approved LTI connection; that setup must be enabled by the Canvas administrator.
